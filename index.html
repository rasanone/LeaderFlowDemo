About Us

The LeaderFlow Development Program currently operates in Houston, Los Angeles, Seattle, Chicago & New York... with plans for continued national expansion!

The application period for the 2026 LeaderFlow Development Program is now closed. We appreciate all who applied and look forward to announcing the next cohort. Please check back for future program updates!

Preparing diverse professionals to lead in the architecture, engineering and construction (AEC) industry

LeaderFlow Development Program

We offer a 7-month curriculum that focuses on personal growth, increased awareness of self and others, strengthened community, and improved skills in management and leadership at no cost to participants. Our integrated program includes the following:

Training & Coaching

Learn vital leadership skills

Personal Growth & Awareness

Community Building

Build connections with industry leaders and other mid-career AEC professionals

Mentorship

Build connections with industry leaders

Continuing Education

Earn credits currently provided through AIA.

Join us and embark on a transformative journey tailored to individuals at all levels of their career. Acquire practice skills, foster personal growth, enhance your professional expertise, accelerate your career advancement, and discover the immense potential within you through our comprehensive program!

Closing the Gap to Advancement

Leadership development training and mentorship to take you further in your personal life and career.
