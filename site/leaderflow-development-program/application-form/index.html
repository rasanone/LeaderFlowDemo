The application is in eight short steps. Your answers are saved on this device as you go, so you can come back and finish later.

Who can apply

  • The LeaderFlow Development Program primarily targets mid-career professionals with a minimum of 5 years of experience and a demonstrated interest and commitment to advancing leadership and innovation within the AEC industry.
  • Applicants must exhibit a demonstrated interest in personal and professional growth.
  • Applicants must commit to in-person attendance at every session. This is a mandatory requirement.

Read the full application process

What you’ll need

  • Your contact details and mailing address.
  • Your current firm or organization, role or title, approximate firm size, and years of experience in the AEC industry.
  • Your LinkedIn profile or professional website (optional).
  • Written answers to six short questions about your leadership goals, your motivation, and how you will meet the program commitment (about 150 to 300 words each). You may want to draft them first.
  • Your resume or CV as a PDF.
  • Your agreement to the program commitments, including in-person attendance at every session.
