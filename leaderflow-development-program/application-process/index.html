Application Process

The LeaderFlow Development Program

The application period for the 2026 LeaderFlow Development Program is now closed. We appreciate all who applied and look forward to announcing the next cohort. Please check back for future program updates!

Eligibility

  • The LeaderFlow Development Program primarily targets mid-career professionals with a minimum of 5 years of experience and a demonstrated interest and commitment to advancing leadership and innovation within the AEC industry.
  • Applicants must exhibit a demonstrated interest in personal and professional growth.
  • Applicants must commit to in-person attendance at every session. This is a mandatory requirement.
  • Applicants must submit a complete online application, which also includes an applicant questionnaire and personal resume.

Required Participant Commitment:

Participants will be expected to fully engage in the following activities:

  • Mandatory in-person attendance at all group sessions. We will provide information to the firm leaders to allocate paid time off as continuing education for all accepted applicants to attend the program during the work week.
  • Preparation for sessions may include some pre-work reading, writing and assessments.
  • Meeting with a “Buddy” between group sessions for accountability and check in.
