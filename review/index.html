leaderflow.org: review and redesign

A page-by-page audit of the live site (11 pages, desktop at 1440px and mobile at 390px), followed by a rebuilt static version that keeps every word of the original copy and replaces the visual design, layout, media handling and code.

Critical: two live pages have been hijacked with gambling spam

/about-us/ and /leaderflow-development-program/participantfaqs/ currently return Korean casino / USDT-exchange spam pages (HTTP 200, served by the site's own server) instead of LeaderFlow content. Both pages are linked from the main navigation and the homepage "About Us" button, so visitors and sponsors clicking "About" land on spam. Search crawlers still receive the normal homepage, which is typical of a targeted SEO-spam compromise of a WordPress install.

  • Tell the hosting provider and run a malware scan / cleanup of the WordPress install.
  • Update WordPress core, the theme and all plugins (Elementor, Revolution Slider, etc.) and remove unused ones.
  • Reset all admin, hosting, FTP and database passwords; review admin users.
  • Request re-indexing in Google Search Console once clean.

For the redesign, the original About and FAQ copy was recovered word for word from the Internet Archive (About: 16 Apr 2026 snapshot, FAQ: 13 Jan 2026 snapshot).

Version 2 · UI overhaul

Learning from larger peer organizations

The first rebuild fixed the problems but kept the old site's layout: dark photo headers with centered white text, pale-blue bands, standard cards. For v2 we studied larger leadership and career nonprofits and borrowed the principles they share. The copy is still word for word.

Career & leadership nonprofits

MLT · SEO · Toigo Foundation · Executive Leadership Council · ALPFA

  • Short statement headlines on a dark navy anchor, with one warm accent color
  • Warm off-white backgrounds instead of pure white (ALPFA #faf9f4)
  • Small uppercase eyebrow labels; sponsors shown by tier
  • Serif + sans pairing (ALPFA uses Lora + Poppins); subtle fade-up motion

Leadership & fellowship programs

Center for Creative Leadership · Braven · Echoing Green · Year Up · Teach For America · Aspen Institute

  • Numbered program steps (Year Up's Train / Experience / Access, TFA's Connect / Lead / Grow)
  • A recognizable graphic device (CCL's network lines over photos)
  • Real people as the hero; consistent photo crops and treatments
  • Navy + cream bands (TFA #00225A / #FFEED4, CCL #fbf8f2)

Award-recognized nonprofit sites

charity: water · Obama Foundation Leaders · Webby nonprofit honorees

  • Premium comes from typographic scale and whitespace, not effects
  • Large editorial pull quotes with clear attribution
  • Every section opens with one idea and ends with one action
  • Motion is restrained and always respects reduced-motion settings

What changed in v2

  1. Editorial type: Fraunces + Inter

    A variable display serif with optical sizing replaces Merriweather; Inter replaces Montserrat's wide 2015-era capitals. Headlines go up to 96px, set tight and left-aligned.

  2. One accent: signal gold

    Gold #f2b33d (from the flag in the logo) is used only for eyebrows, key words, numerals and the Donate button. On light backgrounds it darkens to #8a5a00 for AA contrast.

  3. Signature device: blueprint grid + flow line

    A faint architectural grid on every navy surface and a gold line that draws itself in, a nod to the AEC audience and the "flow" in the name.

  4. Statement hero with an arched photo

    Split layout on deep navy; "Leadership" in gold italic; the photo sits in an arch with an offset gold outline.

  5. Numbered structures

    The five pillars become a bento grid with outlined numerals; the seven sessions become a 01–07 timeline whose connector grows as you scroll.

  6. Consistent people treatment

    Headshots share a navy duotone that resolves to full color on hover; founders get arched portraits.

  7. Sponsor ladder

    Sponsor levels read like a pricing table: tier and pledge on the left, logos (greyscale until hovered) on the right, with Foundational raised on navy.

  8. Editorial quotes and statements

    The city sentence becomes a large statement with gold-underlined cities; the Flowcilitator testimonial becomes a full-width italic pull quote.

Findings and what changed

Homepage hero

Problems

  • Revolution Slider rotates three different headlines (one slide is duplicated), so each visitor sees a random message and none are readable before they change.
  • Headline set in light grey (#777) instead of the brand navy; weak contrast and no hierarchy.
  • On mobile ~400px of empty white space appears above the hero and the subtitle is dropped.
  • The "TrustedSite" badge floats over content on every page.

 

Redesign

  • One static hero: "Accelerate Your Leadership". The other slide messages ("Closing the Gap to Advancement", "Connect & Grow with Us") became their own sections, so no copy is lost.
  • Navy serif headline, readable body copy, one primary button.
  • Stacks cleanly on mobile; badge removed.

Video

Problems

  • The Vimeo embed is set to autoplay=1&muted=0. Browsers block autoplay with sound, so visitors see a large empty black box directly under the headline.
  • The promo MP4 is embedded twice (desktop and mobile copies); its poster image has a fake play icon baked in.
  • Announcements are set as giant centered H1/H2s with a text-shadow.

 

Redesign

  • Click-to-play video facades: a poster image and a real play button. The Vimeo player and the MP4 load only when clicked, with sound, and never autoplay.
  • The MP4 appears once, with a clean poster frame taken from the video.
  • The "application period is closed" notice is now a styled callout, reused consistently on every page that shows it.

Program cards & animation

Problems

  • The "integrated program" cards are a carousel: desktop shows 3 of 5, so Mentorship and Continuing Education are hidden. Mobile shows a different set with different titles.
  • Fixed-height cards with around 60% dead space; one card has no description at all.
  • Three overlapping motion systems (Revolution Slider, CoBlocks animation, a scroll-hijacking SmoothScroll.js) plus four Owl carousels and flip-box hover cards that don't work on touch screens.

 

Redesign

  • All five items shown at once in a numbered, equal-height grid.
  • Program "What is Offered?" flip boxes became cards with an expandable "Program Objectives" list that works with touch and keyboard.
  • The only motion is a subtle fade-in on scroll, turned off for people who prefer reduced motion. Native scrolling is untouched.

Sponsors & logos

Problems

  • Logos range from huge (Aldridge) to unreadable (Building Ventures, Hensel Phelps, CO Architects) because the source images have uneven white margins.
  • Regional, Gold and Silver tiers sit in a left column while Partner Organizations floats on the right with empty space beneath.

 

Redesign

  • Logos trimmed and sized by visual area, so wide and square marks carry similar weight, each in a uniform tile.
  • Clear tier headers (with pledge amounts on the Sponsor page) and an aligned two-column layout for regional sponsors and partners.

Graduates & people

Problems

  • 177 full-size images (~13.5 MB) load at once; the page is 15,236px tall on desktop and 33,365px on mobile.
  • Inconsistent photo crops and sizes; testimonial carousels.
  • Meet the Flowcilitators: uneven rows, mixed cut-out and photo headshots at different sizes.

 

Redesign

  • Cohort tabs (2025 → 2021), each with city accordions; 5-up grid on desktop, 2-up on mobile.
  • All headshots resized to WebP with a uniform 4:5 crop, lazy-loaded only when a city is opened.
  • Testimonials shown as static quote cards; one even grid for Flowcilitators.

Typography, color & consistency

Problems

  • Three font families requested (Montserrat, Merriweather, Roboto), with font CSS loaded twice.
  • More than 20 heading sizes across the site; an H3 at 50px is larger than the H2s around it.
  • Headings switch between grey and navy at random; body copy is ~14px light grey.
  • At least 4 button styles (asymmetric radius, light-blue with a mismatched border, white pill…).
  • Every interior page opens with the same dark, low-contrast photo banner with centered text.

 

Redesign

  • Fraunces display headings and Inter body text on one fluid type scale; ~17px body copy with AA contrast.
  • Headings are always ink navy; one pill button system (navy, gold, ghost) used everywhere.
  • Shorter interior page headers: left-aligned title and breadcrumb on a blueprint-grid navy band, with an angled photo panel.
  • Every image has alt text, there is one H1 per page, form fields have visible labels, and the menu works with the keyboard.

Design system

One stylesheet (assets/css/site.css) and one script (assets/js/site.js), with no frameworks or plugins.

ColorInk #0b2447 for headings and dark bands, brand navy #003f87 for links and buttons, one accent gold #f2b33d (text on light: #8a5a00), warm paper #f7f4ee, sky #7cc4ff for focus on dark, body text #3d4a5c

Copy notes

All visible text was kept word for word. An automated check compares every text block on each new page against the original page source. Nothing new appears except accessibility labels ("Skip to content" and the screen-reader name of the menu button). The only original strings left out are three alternate mobile-only headings that repeated desktop content ("Preparing professionals to lead…", "Questions? Ask!", "Friends of LeaderFlow").

These existing copy issues were left unchanged, as instructed, for the owner to review:

  • FAQ: the last answer is cut off mid-word ("…align your current role with your long-term career vis").
  • Homepage: "Acquire practice skills" (probably "practical skills").
  • Graduates: labels mix "Field:" and "Industry:" for the same information.
  • Footer: "© 2023" is out of date.
  • FAQ: the 2026 application window closes "January 4, 2026", while hidden text on the Application Process page says "January 9, 2026". Applications are closed either way, but the FAQ still describes the window as open.

Next steps

  1. Clean up the WordPress compromise on the live domain (see the security notice above).
  2. Connect the forms. The Contact and Flowcilitator forms keep every field and label, but GitHub Pages has no backend. Set each form's data-endpoint to a form service such as Formspree, Basin or Getform (see README).
  3. Review the copy notes above, then make wording updates in one pass.
  4. Decide where it will live. The site can stay a static site (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages) or be ported into a lean WordPress theme using the same CSS.
